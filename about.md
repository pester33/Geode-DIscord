# GD Discord Presence

Shows what you're doing in Geometry Dash on your Discord profile:

- **Playing a level:** level name and creator, your best %, attempt count and how long you've been playing it
- **GD Gaming List levels** show their rank, like **GDGL #12**, with the GDGL logo
- **Editing:** "Editing a level" with the level name
- **Menus, browsing and level pages** too

Needs the **Discord desktop app** open on the same PC (Windows only).

In the settings you can turn it off, hide level names or progress, or use your own Discord Application ID.
